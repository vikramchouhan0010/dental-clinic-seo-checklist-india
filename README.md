# Dental Clinic SEO Checklist: Treatment Pages, Profile and Trust Signals
Patients searching "root canal specialist open today" are close to booking, so the aim is enquiries, not vanity traffic. Most clinics lose leads for predictable reasons: one generic services page, an under-filled Google Business Profile and thin trust signals.
### Checklist
1. Map keywords to how patients search: "root canal treatment", not "endodontic therapy"; "invisible braces cost", not "clear aligner therapy".
2. Give each treatment its own page: implants, braces, aligners, root canal, whitening, emergency care. Do not stuff them onto one page.
3. On each page explain who it is for, the steps, number of visits, recovery, price factors and the next action.
4. Show the dentist's qualifications, specialties and clinic photos; name the equipment you actually use.
5. Choose the most specific accurate primary category on your Google Business Profile and keep name, address and phone identical on the site and in listings.
6. Ask every patient for a review in the same neutral way; never offer incentives.
7. Write cost, comparison and recovery articles that link to the treatment pages.
